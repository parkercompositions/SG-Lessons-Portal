Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

The Lesson Studio is the private lessons portal run by Sarah Griego and Mitchell Weber for their students, families and partner music store. This page explains what it keeps and why.

What we keep

  • Student details your teacher enters: name, instrument, email (and a parent's email if given), lesson time and rate.
  • Lesson records: lesson dates, progress on scales and repertoire, notes, requests for extra lessons, and whether each lesson has been paid.
  • Get to Know session bookings made through our music store partner: the student's name, a contact email, and any note left with the booking.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address. We use the email to match you to your student record, and nothing else. We never see your Google password.

Google Classroom

Our teachers connect their own Google Classroom account with read-only access, so the portal can show students their class materials and assignments. The portal never changes, posts to or deletes anything in Google Classroom. Data from Google is used only to show it to that class's students and teachers inside this portal. It is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising, and its use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Payments

Payments go through PayPal or Zelle directly. The portal never sees or stores card or bank details. It only records that you said a payment was sent and when your teacher marked it received.

Emails

The portal sends emails about lessons: confirmations, cancellations, away dates, remote lessons and payment reminders. There is no marketing email.

Who can see what

Students and parents see only their own record. Other students appear on the calendar only as anonymous busy times. Teachers see their own students. Our music store partner sees open times and the sessions it booked, never other students' details. We do not sell or share your information.

Where it's stored, and deleting it

Data is stored securely with our hosting provider, Netlify, with daily backups kept for 30 days. To see, correct or delete your information, just ask your teacher or email us. When a student leaves, their sign-in ends right away.
